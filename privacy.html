Privacy policy

Last updated September 11, 2026.

What this static demonstration collects

This website is a static demonstration hosted on GitHub Pages. Standard web-server logs (such as IP address, browser type, and page requested) may be collected by the hosting provider in the ordinary course of serving pages; NicoMach does not operate its own analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising scripts on this site.

What it does not collect

  • No account creation, login, or authentication of any kind.
  • No cookies are set by NicoMach for tracking or advertising purposes.
  • No invoice, financial, or counterparty data is uploaded to this site — every dataset shown is synthetic and pre-loaded into the page.

Sample data is synthetic

All company names, invoice identifiers, amounts, and settlement figures shown in the homepage worked example, the interactive demonstration, and the ledger analysis page are synthetic. They do not describe a real business, transaction, or customer relationship.

The pilot request form

The pilot request page is a static demonstration form. Because this site has no connected backend, submitting the form does not transmit or store the information entered anywhere — nothing leaves your browser. The page states this plainly at the point of submission. If a form backend is connected in a future version of this site, this policy will be updated to describe what is collected and how it is used before that change goes live.

Future production data handling

If NicoMach begins handling real invoice, counterparty, or financial data as part of a pilot or production engagement, that handling will be governed by a separate written agreement — including a data-processing and security addendum, defined retention periods, and purpose limitations — executed directly with each customer before any data is transferred. This website's privacy policy does not itself authorize or describe that future handling.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed through the pilot request page or the contact information on the About page.
