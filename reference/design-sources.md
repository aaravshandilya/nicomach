# Where the design comes from

Sourced from **siteinspire.com → Business & Finance**.

## Hazeltree — the hero
`siteinspire.com/website/12424-hazeltree` → hazeltree.com
Published on siteinspire 28 May 2024. Category: Business & Finance.

Treasury and liquidity management for private markets — the closest sector
match to NicoMach on the site. What was taken:

- **Lead with the system.** Their hero is a schematic of the product itself, not
  an illustration or a hero image. NicoMach's hero is now the settlement flow:
  obligations in, netting, settlement plan out.
- **One accent phrase.** Their headline sets "private markets" in a gold serif
  against sans. NicoMach's sets "liquidity." the same way.
- **Figures under the fold line.** They run real data directly beneath the hero.
  NicoMach runs 14 → 4, 81%, −71% in a strip across the foot.

What was left: their product screenshots and bar chart — NicoMach has no
shipped dashboard to photograph, and a mocked one would be a fake claim.

## UC Investments — the restraint
`siteinspire.com/website/13613-uc-investments` → invest.universityofcalifornia.edu
Sons & Co. Categories: Black & White, Minimal, Business & Finance, Annual Reports.

Pure black, one flat white geometric mark, enormous negative space, type reduced
to a two-line label and a small institutional lockup. Not copied, but it settled
the argument that authority in this sector comes from restraint rather than
ornament — which is why every gradient, halo and glow was removed from this
build.
