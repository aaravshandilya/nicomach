§ The problem

Businesses optimize everything except the obligations between them.

Companies commonly process invoices one at a time. Across a connected business network, some payables and receivables may offset, but fragmented systems, contractual restrictions, and separate approval processes make those opportunities difficult to identify.

Netting does not erase economic obligations or create free money. It preserves what each participant ultimately owes or is owed while potentially reducing the gross settlement activity required to reach those final positions.

§ A worked example

Three obligations. Sixty dollars moved. Zero-dollar net position.

This example assumes that every obligation is verified, eligible, in the same currency, due in the same settlement period, and approved for netting.

    § Designed to minimize the trust required
    I.

    Read-only first

    NicoMach initially analyzes historical or approved invoice data. It does not hold or transfer customer funds.

    II.

    Minimum necessary data

    Use only the fields required for validation, eligibility, reconciliation, and optimization.

    III.

    Participant-controlled approval

    No recommendation becomes an approved settlement without authorized participant review.

    IV.

    Explainable and auditable

    Every result preserves invoice-level lineage, eligibility decisions, engine version, approval history, and reconciliation evidence.
