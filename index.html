The problem

Businesses optimize everything except the obligations between them.

Companies commonly process invoices one at a time. Across a larger network, some obligations may offset, but fragmented systems and independent payment processes make these opportunities difficult to identify.

1

Fragmented obligations

Invoice data is separated across businesses, systems, and payment schedules.

2

Unnecessary movement

Companies may send multiple gross payments when a smaller net settlement could preserve the same final positions.

3

Trapped liquidity

Payment timing, prefunding, fees, and reconciliation can place unnecessary pressure on working capital.

Not all working-capital inefficiency comes from invoice netting — this is one contributing factor among several.

Trust & security

Designed to minimize the trust required.

Read-only first

NicoMach initially analyzes and recommends. It does not hold or transfer funds.

Minimum necessary data

Use only the invoice fields required to determine eligibility and calculate settlement.

Customer-controlled approval

No recommendation becomes a settlement without participant review and approval.

Explainable and auditable

Show how every result was calculated and which obligations contributed to it.

Production deployments will require independent security review, appropriate compliance controls, and trusted financial partners.

Market context

Working capital is one of the world's largest underused assets.

€0.00T

PwC estimates that €1.84 trillion in excess working capital could potentially be released globally.

This estimate includes inventory, receivables, payables, and other working-capital inefficiencies. NicoMach addresses only the portion connected to eligible business obligations and settlement.
