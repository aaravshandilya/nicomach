I. The Brief

A business and product explanation, written plainly.

What NicoMach analyzes, what it recommends, and where the current product boundary sits.

The problem

Businesses commonly treat each invoice as a separate payment instruction, even when a connected set of companies is moving money in a loop. Processing every obligation gross creates more payment instructions and fees, more reconciliation events and exception handling, temporary prefunding or liquidity pressure, and poor visibility into a network's true net positions.

The thesis

"Eligible obligations between independent companies should be analyzed as a network before the remaining payments are routed or financed."

Netting already exists within some corporate groups, through bilateral and multilateral arrangements among commonly controlled entities. NicoMach's hypothesis is extending the analytical and coordination layer to independent businesses — companies that share counterparties but not ownership.

What the product does

StepAction
1Import approved invoice data.
2Validate the records.
3Exclude disputed or restricted invoices.
4Calculate each participant's net position.
5Construct a reduced settlement recommendation.
6Explain which invoices contributed to every result.
7Collect participant approval.
8Export an evidence package.

What the product does not do

Not part of the current product

NicoMach does not currently hold funds, transmit money, guarantee settlement, lend, perform foreign exchange, or independently determine whether an obligation is legally nettable.

  • No custody of customer funds
  • No money transmission
  • No settlement guarantees
  • No lending or invoice financing
  • No foreign exchange
  • No unilateral legal determinations

Initial customer

The current customer hypothesis is controllers, treasury leaders, and finance-operations teams at mid-sized or large companies with recurring counterparties and meaningful invoice volume. The best initial industry has not yet been validated; customer discovery is expected to identify the vertical rather than branding assumptions.

Initial wedge

The first commercial product is read-only historical analysis. A customer supplies synthetic or permissioned historical invoice data, and NicoMach returns an explainable before-and-after settlement comparison. This lets a customer measure potential value before changing any part of its payment process.

Commercial model

The current hypothesis is annual enterprise SaaS pricing based on invoice volume, network complexity, and analytical value. A working figure of approximately $50,000 per year is useful for testing enterprise willingness to pay — it is not validated pricing, and NicoMach makes no representation about what a customer would ultimately be charged.
